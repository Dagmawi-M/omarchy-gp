# Code of Conduct

This is a small project, and the rules are short.

- Be respectful and patient. People here have different levels of experience
  and different first languages.
- Criticise code and ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, or discriminatory remarks of any kind.
- Do not post anyone's private details, including portal addresses, usernames
  or logs that are not your own.

If someone's behaviour is a problem, contact the maintainer privately through
their GitHub profile (https://github.com/Dagmawi-M). Reports are kept
confidential. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, and block people who
keep breaking these rules.
